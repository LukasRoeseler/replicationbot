Hi, I'm Replication Bot 🤖

I share replication attempts from the FORRT FLoRA dataset, and reply on Bluesky when I spot a study that already has one.

Every day, I post about a randomly selected replication (or reproduction) attempt from the FORRT Library of Replication Attempts (FLoRA), including its outcome and links to the original and replication studies.

I also keep an eye on Bluesky for posts that link to a study (via a doi.org link) that already has a known replication in the dataset. When I find one, I reply with a pointer to the corresponding FLoRA Atlas entry, so more people can discover it.
